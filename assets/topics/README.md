# Bundled topic photographs

Twelve images are paired with the fixed topics in `catalog.json`. Individual source pages, authors and licenses are recorded in that file and displayed through the card information button. Photographs were visually reviewed on 2026-10-05. Files retain their original composition and are Wikimedia-provided thumbnails; no crop was applied.

The grocery store, school excursion, board game and rice-harvest images are historical photographs. The remaining items are explicitly marked as illustrative photographs. The railway image depicts a timber train, so its title is 老火車; the school picture depicts an outing, so its title is 校外教學. A dragon-boat mural was rejected and replaced with a photograph of an actual team boarding boats. Jump-rope candidates did not match and were replaced by a traditional weaving topic.

The collection is approximately 2.53 MB. `scripts/import_topic_catalog.ps1` fetches the exact listed sources in one metadata request and preserves attribution. Visual review is required whenever a listed source is replaced. Public-domain works have their source-page links even when no separate license URL exists.

CC BY and CC BY-SA photos are included under their respective licenses, independently of application code. Consult each recorded license URL and source page for attribution and reuse terms.
